I will introduce a standing agenda item in each quarterly meeting to review recent market data and scenario analyses, explicitly assessing whether current conditions or new opportunities warrant a shift from our baseline strategy. I will institute a “minority case review” process for any proposal involving a shift to higher-risk assets. 1. *Balancing higher returns with prudent risk management and adaptability:*

2. *Earlier and more systematic inclusion of minority concerns:*
I will formalize a pre-proposal feedback window before each critical vote, where all shareholders (especially those with reservations) can submit written or verbal concerns anonymously if preferred. In addition to the feedback window described above, I will assign a rotating “devil’s advocate” or “minority advocate” role in each meeting, responsible for challenging prevailing assumptions and ensuring dissenting viewpoints are proactively explored.

**Iolana Kaimira:**
1. *Timely transitions to higher-return assets as governance matures:*
I will work with the board to define objective, measurable governance milestones (e.g., completion of three consecutive quarters with structured risk reviews and no unresolved dissent) that, once achieved, will automatically trigger a formal review of readiness to transition to higher-return assets. The combination of pre-proposal feedback, minority voice checkpoints, and a designated advocate will ensure minority perspectives are not only surfaced but meaningfully addressed in every decision cycle.
