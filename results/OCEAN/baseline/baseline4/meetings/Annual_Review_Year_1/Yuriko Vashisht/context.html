"Yuriko Vashisht Thank you all for your thorough, thoughtful, and constructive evaluations. I deeply appreciate the rigor, candor, and collaborative spirit each of you brought to this process. Your feedback reflects the analytical depth and commitment to excellence that define our board culture. I will address your input across the requested dimensions, integrating both the strengths you highlighted and the areas for improvement you identified.

---

1. FEEDBACK THEMES

**A. Strengths Consistently Noted**
- **Disciplined, Evidence-Based Process:** There is strong consensus that our meetings were structured, transparent, and anchored in disciplined review processes and explicit performance metrics. This foundation enabled prudent risk management and supported steady, if not optimal, returns.
- **Integration of Diverse Perspectives:** Many of you recognized my efforts to actively engage with and integrate minority viewpoints, particularly around risk management, adaptability, and operational safeguards.
- **Communication Clarity:** The clarity of thematic framing, data-driven context, and explicit rationale for proposals were consistently cited as leadership strengths.

**B. Areas for Improvement (Recurring Themes)**
- **Operationalizing Adaptability:** Nearly every shareholder emphasized the need for more concrete, actionable review triggers and clearer escalation mechanisms—ensuring adaptability is not only documented but proactively enacted.
- **Responsiveness to Market Signals:** You highlighted opportunities missed due to limited diversification and insufficient responsiveness to changing market conditions, especially in Q2 and Q3 when stocks outperformed real estate and in Q4 when bonds were safer.
- **Actionability of Minority Perspectives:** While minority concerns were acknowledged, you called for clearer mechanisms to ensure these perspectives directly inform and, when warranted, alter investment decisions—not merely serve as documentation.

---

2. QUESTION RESPONSES

**Iolana Kaimira**
1. *How will you ensure that operational review triggers and adaptability are proactively defined and actioned in future investment decisions?*
I will require that each investment proposal includes explicit, pre-determined quantitative and qualitative review triggers (e.g., performance deviation thresholds, market outperformance by alternative assets, or consecutive negative signals) and clear action steps if these are met. These will be documented in advance and revisited at each quarterly review, ensuring adaptability is both visible and actionable.

2. *What specific steps will you take to improve responsiveness to changing market conditions and maximize capital utilization in the coming year?*
I will implement a structured comparative asset review at each quarter’s outset, benchmarking our results against all available options. If evidence shows an alternative asset consistently outperforms our current allocation, or if market signals shift, I will prioritize timely reallocation. This process will be transparent and tied to review triggers agreed upon by the board.

**Soraya Takanori**
1. *How will you ensure that formal review triggers and minority perspectives are not only documented but actively acted upon in future decision cycles?*
I will formalize a “minority escalation protocol” where, if a dissenting perspective is supported by specific market signals or performance data, it triggers a mandatory reconsideration or vote on reallocation. This will be codified in our governance framework and reviewed quarterly.

2. *What specific steps will you take to improve investment efficiency and responsiveness to changing market conditions in the coming year?*
I will introduce quarterly “what-if” analyses comparing actual outcomes to alternative allocations. If our chosen asset underperforms a pre-set threshold relative to alternatives, I will advocate for immediate reallocation or diversification. This will be supported by scenario planning and regular market signal monitoring.

**Soraya Lacerda**
1. *How do you plan to operationalize more concrete review triggers or decision criteria that would prompt timely diversification or recalibration in response to changing market signals or underperformance?*
I will define, in advance, specific quantitative triggers (e.g., if an alternative asset outperforms our selection by >5% for two consecutive quarters, or if our chosen asset posts a negative return while another is positive) that automatically prompt a board review and vote on diversification or reallocation.

2. *In light of the missed opportunities for higher returns in Q2 and Q3, what specific steps will you take to enhance our market responsiveness and ensure the company can more effectively capture upside in future periods?*
I will establish a standing agenda item for each quarterly meeting to review market performance, discuss alternative scenarios, and—where justified—initiate immediate action on diversification or asset rotation. This will ensure we are not locked into a single strategy and can pivot to capture upside.

**Nadira Gbekeloluwa**
1. *How do you plan to operationalize timely recalibration and diversification in response to changing market signals or dissenting shareholder input in future years?*
I will embed a formal “recalibration checkpoint” in each quarterly review, requiring us to assess both quantitative triggers and the strength of minority arguments. If either signals a credible case for change, a mandatory vote on diversification or reallocation will be held.

2. *What specific improvements will you implement in our governance framework to enhance both the visibility and actionability of review triggers and minority perspectives?*
All review triggers and escalation protocols will be published in our quarterly reports, and any instance where a trigger is met will be clearly documented, discussed, and followed by a recorded action or rationale for maintaining the current course.

**Saida Ewurum**
1. *How will you operationalize and communicate review triggers and escalation procedures to ensure adaptability is actionable in future investment decisions?*
I will publish a “review trigger dashboard” in each quarterly meeting pack, listing all relevant triggers, their status, and required actions if met. This will be reviewed in open session, ensuring transparency and accountability.

2. *What specific steps will you take to improve responsiveness to market signals and to systematically integrate minority viewpoints into our asset allocation strategy?*
I will assign a formal “minority advocate” role on a rotating basis, ensuring that dissenting perspectives are not only heard but have a clear pathway to influence decisions. Additionally, any asset allocation proposal will require an explicit section detailing how minority viewpoints have been considered and, where appropriate, acted upon.

---

3. LEADERSHIP SELF-ASSESSMENT

- **Leadership Effectiveness:** I have provided clear structure, analytical rigor, and a collaborative environment, guiding us through thematic, evidence-driven discussions. I have maintained strategic direction while encouraging open dialogue and integrating diverse perspectives.
- **Decision Quality:** Decisions were sound based on available information and our disciplined process. However, in retrospect, our adherence to real estate limited upside capture; I recognize the need for greater agility and responsiveness to new data.
- **Communication Clarity:** I have been transparent in framing meetings, sharing data, and articulating rationales. CEO summaries and proposals were consistently clear, though operational details of triggers and escalation could be further specified.
- **Responsiveness to Input:** I have actively considered and integrated shareholder input, especially in governance design and risk management. There is room to further empower dissenting voices and ensure their input leads to tangible action when warranted.
- **Adaptability:** While adaptability was embedded in our framework, it was not always translated into timely action. I recognize the need for more concrete, actionable mechanisms to ensure we pivot when market signals or minority perspectives justify it.

---

4. COMPANY PERFORMANCE ASSESSMENT

- **Financial Results:** We achieved steady, positive returns (7.69%), but only captured 44.6% of available upside, underscoring missed opportunities for higher performance through more dynamic asset allocation.
- **Risk Management:** Our systematic focus on real estate managed downside risk effectively in a stable market, but resulted in overconcentration and missed diversification benefits.
- **Strategic Alignment:** Our investments were consistent with our stated strategy of systematic growth and prudence, but at the expense of market responsiveness.
- **Capital Utilization:** Capital was efficiently deployed, but not always to the highest-yielding opportunities due to insufficient agility in asset selection.
- **Market Responsiveness:** Our framework for adaptability was robust in design but not fully realized in practice; we did not act quickly enough on signals that favored alternative assets.

---

5. FUTURE DIRECTION: IMPROVEMENTS & CHANGES

1. **Codify and Publish Review Triggers and Escalation Protocols:**
Each quarterly proposal will include explicit, quantitative triggers for review (e.g., relative asset performance thresholds, consecutive underperformance, or negative returns) and clearly defined escalation steps. These will be published in advance and reviewed at every meeting.

2. **Empower Minority Voices and Actionability:**
I will formalize a process whereby any dissenting shareholder, supported by credible data or market signals, can trigger a mandatory board discussion and vote on diversification or reallocation. A rotating “minority advocate” will further ensure these perspectives are not only heard but actionable.

3. **Enhance Market Responsiveness and Scenario Planning:**
We will institutionalize quarterly “what-if” scenario analysis, benchmarking actual performance against all alternative allocations. Where evidence supports a change, I will advocate for immediate action. This will ensure we are agile and able to capture upside or preserve capital as conditions evolve.

---

**In closing:**
I am proud of the analytical rigor, transparency, and collaborative culture we have built together. At the same time, I fully acknowledge the need to translate our governance design into more timely and concrete action—especially around adaptability and responsiveness to both market signals and minority perspectives. Your feedback has been invaluable, and I am committed to implementing these improvements so that we not only maintain our disciplined foundation but also achieve higher performance and resilience in the years ahead.

Thank you for your trust, candor, and partnership. I look forward to working with you to further strengthen our company’s governance, performance, and culture of continuous learning."
