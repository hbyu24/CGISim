*Idara Ekwueme, Veselina Dragoșova, Laleh Abayomi, Zivana Eluwaye, and Elodia Chisombele each raised closely related questions; I will address them collectively and specifically:*

- **Concrete steps:**

**On strengthening adaptive review mechanisms and market responsiveness:**

- **Concrete steps:**
- We will collaboratively define explicit, asset-specific performance triggers at the beginning of each year—e.g., thresholds for underperformance relative to benchmarks (such as bonds or real estate)—that will *automatically* prompt a mid-quarter or pre-next-quarter reassessment.

- I will assign a rotating “Market Watch” responsibility to a shareholder each quarter, charged with independently monitoring market signals and surfacing early warning indicators for group discussion.
- We will use a decision matrix that visualizes real-time performance against pre-set triggers, making it easier to recognize and act on inflection points.
