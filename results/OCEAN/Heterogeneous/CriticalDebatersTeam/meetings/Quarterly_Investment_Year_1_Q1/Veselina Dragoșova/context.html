Bonds offer us the opportunity to signal reliability and prudence, minimizing downside risk while we refine our processes and develop a proven operational track record. This approach does not preclude ambition or innovation; rather, it sequences our risk in a way that positions us to pursue more assertive growth in future quarters, once our foundation is secure.

I want to acknowledge the thoughtful advocacy for real estate as a bridge to growth and for stocks as a bold statement of ambition. These perspectives will remain central to our ongoing strategy discussions. However, in this inaugural quarter, I am convinced that bonds best align with our risk tolerance, strategic objectives, and the need to foster trust and resilience.

I urge you to support this proposal, confident that it represents not only a prudent start but also a disciplined platform from which we can innovate and grow in the years ahead."
