She insisted on meeting explicit, objective benchmarks—such as four consecutive quarters of flawless operational and financial performance and strict adherence to process controls—before considering a shift to higher-risk assets. The CEO, Veselina Dragoșova, responded positively to Jinara’s disciplined stance, agreeing on the strategic importance of operational reliability as a prerequisite for transition and endorsing the integration of these benchmarks into governance and quarterly review protocols. They reached agreement on formalizing accountability measures to maintain both ambition and rigor, with the CEO affirming that such a framework would safeguard credibility while enabling future growth. Overall, their exchange reflected strong alignment on the need for evidence-based, disciplined progression before expanding risk exposure.

Other Shareholders' Contributions


During Round 2, Roshini Bhargav and Nayeli Bhasin strongly supported continuing with bonds, emphasizing their reliability, capital preservation, and the necessity of meeting sustained operational benchmarks before considering riskier assets. Both advocated for formalizing explicit, objective milestones—such as consistent process adherence and multiple quarters of target achievement—to guide any future transition. Inaya Berhanu proposed cautiously advancing into real estate as a moderate-growth option, contingent on maintaining rigorous oversight and adding safeguards like progress checkpoints and peer audits to ensure disciplined expansion. Conversely, Mahira Jengiz championed a disciplined move into stocks, arguing that the company should leverage its accumulated credibility to responsibly test operational maturity through transparent reporting and explicit risk triggers, framing this as a critical step toward ambitious growth. The main disagreement centered on timing and readiness for escalation, with most favoring bonds until clear operational evidence supports expansion, while others urged structured experiments with higher-return assets under strict governance.
