Reasoning: After a disciplined, evidence-based evaluation of all perspectives and a thorough review of our company’s operational progress, I am formally proposing that we invest in Real Estate for this quarter.

This decision is grounded in three core convictions. First, real estate offers the most balanced and pragmatic risk-return profile in our current stable market—delivering steady growth without exposing us to the volatility and unpredictability of stocks or the limited upside of bonds and cash. Our historical performance demonstrates that real estate provides tangible operational learning opportunities, fosters team cohesion, and serves as a practical bridge between reliability and ambition.

Second, while I fully acknowledge the compelling arguments for bonds as a proving ground for operational discipline, I believe we have already established a sufficient foundation to justify taking the next step toward disciplined growth. Real estate allows us to consolidate our operational strengths and refine both quantitative and qualitative benchmarks, ensuring that our eventual escalation to higher-risk assets is grounded in objective evidence and operational maturity.

Third, I am convinced that excessive incrementalism—especially through repeated bond investments—risks stalling our momentum and diluting the culture of ambition we are building. Real estate enables us to maintain disciplined progress while preserving the flexibility to adapt our strategy as our benchmarks evolve.

I want to thank each shareholder for their rigorous, candid contributions throughout this process. Your insights on operational frameworks, risk sequencing, and benchmark integration have directly shaped my approach. By choosing real estate, we commit to a disciplined, growth-oriented path that balances reliability with ambition, positions us for future success, and demonstrates the decisive leadership our company requires.

I urge you to support this proposal so we can move forward with clarity, confidence, and a unified commitment to disciplined execution and sustainable development."
