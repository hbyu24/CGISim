1. FEEDBACK THEMES

**Theme 1: Consensus-Building & Decision Agility**
A recurring theme across your feedback is the need for more robust, timely consensus-building and greater decisiveness, particularly in the face of persistent division. Several of you noted that while our multi-round, inclusive process enabled deep exploration of perspectives, it sometimes resulted in protracted debates and delayed alignment—most notably in Q3 and Q4. There is a clear call to refine our mechanisms for surfacing, integrating, and reconciling differing viewpoints earlier, so we can accelerate high-quality decisions and minimize reliance on executive authority.

**Theme 2: Integration of Minority and Risk-Managed Perspectives**
Many of you highlighted the importance of more proactively incorporating minority, cautious, and risk-managed perspectives—not just acknowledging them, but ensuring they substantively shape our final proposals and decisions. While I strove to create an inclusive environment, the feedback suggests we can do better at bridging the gap between ambition and caution, especially when divisions persist.

**Theme 3: Capitalizing on Market Opportunities & Adaptability**
Almost all of you noted that, despite our disciplined process, we captured only 7.6% of the maximum possible returns this year. This points to a need for sharper adaptability and more dynamic use of real-time market data. Several comments referenced missed upside (e.g., Q3 and Q4 asset selection) and urged a more responsive approach to emerging performance signals—one that enables us to pivot confidently and maximize returns while still honoring risk management.

2. RESPONSES TO SPECIFIC QUESTIONS

**Mahira Jengiz:**
1. *How do you plan to refine the consensus-building process to enable more timely and decisive investment decisions in future years?*
I will introduce structured pre-vote alignment sessions, where we proactively identify and address major objections before proposals are finalized. This will include facilitated “minority voice” rounds and the use of real-time polling to surface shifts in sentiment early. Additionally, I will establish clearer criteria for when we escalate to executive decision-making, ensuring it is a last resort rather than a default.

2. *What specific changes will you make to ensure the company more effectively captures available upside and adapts to real-time market performance data?*
I will implement quarterly “look-back” reviews to compare our decisions against actual market outcomes, and introduce explicit “pivot triggers” based on performance thresholds. This will allow us to adapt our strategy more nimbly in response to evolving data, and ensure we are not anchored in prior assumptions when conditions shift.

**Vesna Branković:**
1. *How do you plan to better integrate minority and cautious shareholder perspectives into final investment decisions, especially when there is ongoing division?*
I will formalize a process where minority and risk-averse perspectives are not only voiced but actively co-developed into hybrid or phased proposals. For example, we can pilot “sequencing” or “blended” strategies that allow us to test higher-risk options with built-in safety nets or staged allocations, directly addressing the concerns of more cautious shareholders.

2. *What specific changes will you make in our feedback and discussion processes next year to improve consensus-building and ensure all voices feel genuinely incorporated?*
We will adopt rotating “devil’s advocate” roles and structured feedback loops after each discussion round, explicitly inviting dissenting views to help refine and stress-test proposals before voting. I will also schedule mid-quarter check-ins to revisit prior decisions and ensure ongoing alignment.

**Idara Ekwueme:**
1. *How do you plan to refine consensus-building and feedback mechanisms to avoid repeated voting deadlocks and accelerate decisive action in future investment cycles?*
I will introduce real-time consensus mapping, where we visually track alignment and divergence on key criteria throughout the discussion. If we see persistent deadlock, we will pause to co-create integrative options—such as staged investments or explicit adaptation thresholds—before moving to a vote.

2. *In light of the company’s low investment efficiency relative to market opportunities, what specific changes will you implement to ensure more effective capital allocation and risk-adjusted decision-making next year?*
I will propose a framework for “adaptive allocation,” where we systematically review market performance each quarter and adjust our asset selection criteria accordingly. This will include pre-defined “pivot points” that trigger shifts in strategy if actual returns deviate from expectations, ensuring we are responsive and disciplined in our capital deployment.

**Jinara Nandini:**
1. *How do you plan to more effectively integrate risk-managed perspectives into future investment proposals to avoid repeated deadlocks and better align with diverse shareholder risk tolerances?*
I will ensure that every proposal includes a “risk management appendix” developed in collaboration with risk-averse shareholders, detailing safeguards, sequencing, and adaptation criteria. This will make risk-managed perspectives a core part of every decision, not just an afterthought.

2. *What specific steps will you take to improve consensus-building and explore hybrid or incremental strategies that balance ambition with operational stability in the coming year?*
I will pilot hybrid proposals—such as partial allocations to higher-risk assets with concurrent exposure to stabilizing assets—and incremental “test-and-learn” approaches, where we commit to bolder moves only after reaching pre-defined confidence thresholds. I will also facilitate joint drafting sessions for proposals to ensure all voices are authentically represented.

**Inaya Berhanu:**
1. *How do you plan to refine our consensus-building process to reduce the frequency of executive decisions and enhance team alignment in future investment rounds?*
I will front-load the identification of potential objections and facilitate collaborative solution-building before formal proposals are made. This will include pre-meeting surveys to surface latent concerns and structured co-design sessions for proposals, aiming to resolve divisions early and minimize the need for executive override.

2. *In light of the relatively low investment efficiency this year, what specific steps will you take to improve our ability to capture greater market opportunities while maintaining psychological safety and team cohesion?*
I will establish quarterly “opportunity audits” to review missed upside and adapt our strategy accordingly. At the same time, I will ensure that all bold moves are paired with clear adaptation criteria and regular check-ins on psychological safety, so we never sacrifice cohesion or trust in pursuit of returns.
