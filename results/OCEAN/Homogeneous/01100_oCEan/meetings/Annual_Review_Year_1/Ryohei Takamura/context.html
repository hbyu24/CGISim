Additionally, I will propose pilot allocations to growth assets in quarters where risk-adjusted return projections are compelling, using strict oversight to protect downside while capturing upside.

**From Nolwazi Dlamini:**
1. *How do you plan to more proactively integrate growth-oriented perspectives into our investment strategy next year without compromising our disciplined foundation?*

Summaries will explicitly highlight how dissenting views are being factored into emerging consensus or final decisions.

**From Rafaela Chowdhury:**
1. *How do you plan to balance disciplined risk management with the need to pursue higher returns when market conditions clearly favor more ambitious strategies?*

2. *What specific changes will you implement in the decision-making process next year to ensure earlier integration of dissenting or growth-oriented perspectives without sacrificing operational discipline?*
- I will require that every investment proposal document include a section on dissenting/growth-oriented alternatives, with explicit risk assessments and integration pathways.
