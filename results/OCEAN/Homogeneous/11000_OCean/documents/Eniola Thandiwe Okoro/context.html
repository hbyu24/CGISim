**5. Future Direction: Specific Improvements**

1. **Institutionalize Early and Structured Dissent**
Every meeting will feature a required dissent/contrarian round and a rotating “risk monitor” or “devil’s advocate” role. This will ensure that minority perspectives are not only welcomed but expected, preventing premature consensus and surfacing blind spots early.

2. **Implement Dynamic Asset Rotation Triggers**
We will adopt explicit, quantitative performance thresholds and scenario-based review protocols. If an asset underperforms by a set margin, the next quarter’s allocation will default to a comparative review with a bias toward the outperformer—unless robustly challenged and overturned.

3. **Enhance Scenario-Based Decision Frameworks**
Each quarter will include a formal “signal review” and “reversal scenario” analysis, comparing realized returns to expectations and modeling the impact of switching allocations. This will embed tactical flexibility and adaptive learning into our operational DNA.
