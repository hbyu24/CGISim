Above all, I will continue to lead with empathy, inclusiveness, and transparency, while holding myself accountable for driving greater organizational discipline and strategic adaptability. I am committed to building on the trust and harmony we have established, but I will also push us—gently but persistently—toward a more balanced approach that honors both our emotional needs and our growth ambitions.
