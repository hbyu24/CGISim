- A documented reduction in volatility or risk associated with stocks, supported by our ongoing market analysis.
- A consensus among shareholders, informed by both quantitative data and qualitative assessment, that the risk-return profile of stocks now aligns with our company’s objectives and risk tolerance.

1. **Proactive Solicitation of Dissent:** Formalize early-stage solicitation of dissenting and alternative perspectives in every discussion round, through structured agenda segments, rotating roles, and anonymous input channels.
