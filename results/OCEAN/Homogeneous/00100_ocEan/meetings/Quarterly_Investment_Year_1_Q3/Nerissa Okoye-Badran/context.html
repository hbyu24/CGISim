Results: 3/6 votes in favor (50.0%). The proposal was REJECTED.

Individual votes:
- Darael Mukendi: Approve - Real estate is the most practical, straightforward option in this stable market—one flat quarter doesn’t change the fact that it delivers visible results and keeps us moving forward. I’m not interested in slowing down for the sake of comfort; disciplined execution and sticking to proven routines will build the credibility we need. Approving this proposal is the clearest way to show we’re serious about growth and capable of handling real challenges.
- Sofiane Qadir: Reject - I do not support investing in real estate for this quarter. The risks of inconsistent execution and the lack of a proven, repeatable track record outweigh the potential for momentum, especially when bonds offer steady, reliable returns with minimal operational complexity. Building credibility through disciplined, predictable results is a more practical and responsible path for our company at this stage.
- Darija Vukadinović: Approve - Real estate is the most practical and straightforward way to keep our growth visible and momentum strong in this stable market. We’ve already proven it delivers results if we stick to routines, and I see no point in slowing down for the sake of caution when the risks are manageable and the upside is clear.
- Nerissa Okoye-Badran: Reject - I do not support investing in real estate for this quarter. The recent flat performance highlights that our operational discipline is still unproven, and I am not willing to risk credibility on momentum alone when bonds offer steady, reliable returns that better fit our current maturity. Until we consistently demonstrate disciplined execution, I believe prioritizing stability and repeatable results is the only practical way forward.
- Suhaila Mendonsa: Reject - I still see no evidence that we’ve built the operational discipline needed for real estate—one good quarter followed by a flat result is not a track record. I’m not interested in risking our capital or credibility just to chase momentum when bonds offer steady, reliable returns and actually fit where we are as a company. Until I see consistent, repeatable results, I won’t support taking on more complexity for the sake of appearances.
- Tafadzwa Ndlovu: Approve - I remain convinced that real estate is the most practical and results-driven choice for this quarter, especially given our strong 10% return last time and the stable market conditions. The concerns about operational discipline are valid, but I believe we can address them through focused, incremental improvements while maintaining momentum—delivering visible progress is essential for our credibility and growth at this stage.


Meeting Conclusion

Response to Failed Vote


After the proposal was rejected, CEO Tafadzwa Ndlovu decided to force through original proposal.
Reason: I have listened carefully to all perspectives and recognize the concerns raised about operational discipline and the preference for a more cautious approach. However, given the stable market and the need to set a clear, decisive tone for our company’s growth, I remain convinced that continuing with real estate is the most practical and results-driven path. My leadership style is direct and action-oriented—I prioritize momentum, visible progress, and learning through real-world execution rather than retreating to safer but less impactful options. While I respect the arguments for reliability, my responsibility as CEO is to act in the company’s best interests as I see them. I am confident that, if we adhere to proven routines and maintain a practical, focused approach, we are capable of managing the operational demands of real estate. This is my final decision for this quarter: we will proceed with investing in real estate, and I expect everyone to commit fully to disciplined execution as we move forward.
