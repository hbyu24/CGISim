2. **Dynamic Market Responsiveness:**
- Formalize a quarterly market signal review and mid-quarter checkpoint process to assess and act on emerging opportunities.
- Justify continued adherence to or deviation from our baseline strategy with scenario-based analysis and explicit debate.

3. **Operational Risk Frameworks from the Outset:**
- Accompany every proposal with a detailed, scenario-based risk management framework, including predefined escalation triggers and transparent reporting.
- Establish a risk protocol dashboard and periodic deep-dives to review activations, near-misses, and protocol effectiveness.

These changes are designed to preserve our disciplined, analytical foundation while fostering greater adaptability, inclusivity, and tactical agility. I remain committed to leading with intellectual rigor, operational clarity, and a willingness to challenge both my own assumptions and those of the group.
