Final Proposal


CEO Samarjit Basnet made the following proposal:

"Vote Proposal: Bonds
Reasoning: After a thorough and disciplined evaluation of both our historical performance and the perspectives shared in this meeting, I am formally proposing that we invest in Bonds for this quarter.

The reasoning is clear and grounded in both evidence and operational experience. Bonds have consistently delivered 5% returns with minimal volatility, directly supporting our company’s priorities of reliable growth, capital protection, and disciplined process execution. This track record has not only produced steady financial gains but has also fostered a culture of operational confidence, reduced internal stress, and strengthened our commitment to process rigor.

Alternative options—real estate and stocks—may offer higher theoretical upside, but their recent volatility and added complexity are incompatible with our current risk tolerance and operational maturity. Cash, while safe, simply does not support the meaningful capital appreciation necessary to meet our growth objectives.

The unanimous support from all shareholders further validates that bonds are the optimal choice in this stable market environment. This decision is not about avoiding risk entirely, but about exercising disciplined judgment and maintaining the operational standards that will enable us to scale sustainably in the future.

By choosing bonds, we reinforce our disciplined, risk-managed culture and position ourselves to capitalize on greater opportunities as our track record develops. I urge you to support this proposal, as it reflects both our collective wisdom and our unwavering commitment to building a resilient foundation for long-term company success."
