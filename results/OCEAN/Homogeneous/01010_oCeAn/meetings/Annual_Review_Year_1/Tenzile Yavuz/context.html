I will personally invite dissenting or minority perspectives at each decision point, making it clear that constructive challenge is valued and essential for our growth. 1. **Active Solicitation of Alternatives:**

2. **Enhancing Responsiveness to Opportunities:**
After each quarter, we will compare our results with those of the highest-yielding asset and discuss whether a different choice would have been justified based on available information at the time. If patterns emerge that suggest prudent opportunities for incremental improvement, we will adapt our approach accordingly—always within our risk tolerance.

*From Ebele Kafando:*

1. **Learning from Missed Opportunities:**

2. **Fostering Interactive Dialogue:**
I will make meetings more interactive by encouraging open debate, inviting each shareholder to question or challenge the consensus, and using small-group breakouts or written prompts to surface a broader range of perspectives.

*From Joana Lirit Afewerki:*
