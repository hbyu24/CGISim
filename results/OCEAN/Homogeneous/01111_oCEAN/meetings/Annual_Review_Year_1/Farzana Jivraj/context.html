You’ve asked for more explicit scenario planning and dynamic adaptation to market signals, especially as the company matures.

**2. Responses to Shareholder Questions:**

*Farzana Jivraj:*

1. **Balancing Stability and Pursuing Higher Returns:**
As we gain experience and our foundation strengthens, I intend to introduce a more structured “opportunity scan” at the start of each investment cycle. This will include a dedicated agenda item where we systematically evaluate not only the risks, but also the upside potential of each asset class, referencing current market data and past performance. I will encourage the group to consider incremental risk-taking in quarters where conditions are favorable and our capital position is strong, while still honoring our commitment to capital preservation and psychological safety.

2. **Surfacing Dissenting or Minority Viewpoints:**
Next year, I will formally invite each shareholder—especially those with alternative or less vocal perspectives—to share their views before we move toward consensus. I will also introduce an explicit “devil’s advocate” segment in our discussions, where we challenge our prevailing assumptions and explore the merits of alternative strategies. This should help ensure all strategic risks and opportunities are fully considered.

1. **Ensuring a Wider Range of Perspectives:**
I will rotate the responsibility for opening the analysis of each asset class among shareholders, not just myself. This will give everyone a platform to introduce their own risk appetite and reasoning, and it may surface perspectives that I, as a routine-oriented leader, might otherwise overlook.

2. **Adapting to Market Changes or Higher-Return Opportunities:**
I will implement a quarterly “market trigger review,” where we explicitly discuss what changes in market conditions or company performance would warrant a shift in strategy. We will predefine clear, objective criteria for considering higher-return assets, so we can respond more quickly and confidently when opportunities arise.

*Nevena Stoyanovic:*

1. **Incorporating Diverse Viewpoints and Avoiding Groupthink:**
I will incorporate a standing agenda item for “alternative scenarios and minority positions” in every meeting, and I will encourage constructive debate by assigning someone each quarter to argue for a less popular or higher-return option. This will help us rigorously test our consensus and ensure we are not overlooking valuable opportunities.

2. **Improving Responsiveness to Market Shifts:**
I will lead the team in developing a simple scenario planning process, where we identify and periodically revisit the conditions under which we would pivot toward more dynamic strategies. By doing this, we can agree in advance on what would trigger a change, making us more agile when those moments arrive.

*Veena Ajitham:*

1. **Encouraging Robust Discussion of Alternatives:**
I will set aside time in each meeting for a “contrarian round,” where we specifically invite challenges to the prevailing consensus and ask the group to consider the risks of not diversifying or missing upside. 2. **Adapting Dynamically to Market Shifts or Opportunities:**
I will establish regular checkpoints—both quarterly and mid-quarter, if needed—to review market data and our portfolio performance. If leading indicators or performance gaps emerge, we will be prepared to revisit our strategy and make timely adjustments.

*Veselina Dobrovolska:*

1. **Balancing Stability with Pursuing Higher Returns:**
My intention is to maintain our disciplined, stability-focused core while introducing a framework for selective risk-taking. For example, we can agree in advance on a small percentage of capital that may be allocated to higher-return assets in quarters where market signals are strong, allowing us to pursue upside without compromising our foundation.

I will personally reach out to less vocal shareholders ahead of meetings to invite their input and concerns. During meetings, I will ensure everyone is given the floor to contribute, and I will rotate facilitation roles for certain agenda items to empower broader participation.
