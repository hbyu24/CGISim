- **Strategic Alignment:** Our decisions were consistent with our stated strategy of operational learning, process discipline, and prudent stewardship. However, we must balance this with greater flexibility to seize emerging opportunities.
- **Capital Utilization:** We efficiently deployed 100% of available capital in line with our approved strategy, supporting robust data collection and learning.
- **Market Responsiveness:** This was our weakest area. We did not sufficiently adjust our asset allocation in response to changing market conditions or performance data, resulting in missed opportunities.

**5. Future Direction: Concrete Improvements for Next Year**

Based on your feedback and my own reflections, I will implement the following changes:

1. **Earlier and More Explicit Scenario Planning:** Every investment cycle will begin with scenario analysis and risk mapping for all asset options, including hybrid and diversified strategies. This will ensure we are considering a full spectrum of outcomes before narrowing options.
2. **Structured Integration of Dissenting Perspectives:** I will formalize opportunities for dissenting shareholders to present alternative or hybrid proposals in each discussion round, and will actively work to integrate these perspectives into final decisions.
3. **Quarterly Adaptability and Diversification Reviews:** We will establish standing agenda items to review recent market performance, assess whether our current strategy remains optimal, and consider asset rotation or diversification as appropriate. Explicit triggers for reconsidering our approach will be set based on objective criteria.
