- I will formalize a process where any shareholder can request a review of alternative asset classes or risk profiles, and these requests will be documented and evaluated in each meeting. If there is persistent or well-supported advocacy for a shift, I will ensure it is given structured discussion time and, where appropriate, reflected in the proposal put to a vote.

2. **What specific steps will you implement to ensure our investment strategy can be more responsive to changing market conditions or emerging opportunities in the coming year?**
- We will adopt a standing review of recent market data and performance in each meeting, with an explicit agenda item for “opportunity identification.” If credible opportunities emerge, we will evaluate them against our readiness criteria and consider incremental reallocation as appropriate.

- I propose we define criteria such as: (a) consecutive quarters where alternative assets outperform bonds by a material margin; (b) consensus among at least two shareholders to revisit risk posture; (c) absence of capital losses over a defined period; and (d) external market signals indicating a shift in risk/reward dynamics. These will be reviewed at each checkpoint and will inform any proposal to incrementally test higher-return assets.
2. **How do you plan to operationalize more proactive integration of divergent perspectives to ensure adaptability without compromising disciplined risk management?**
- I will formalize a “minority integration protocol,” where dissenting proposals are documented, discussed, and—if they meet a threshold of support—piloted in parallel or as a phased allocation. This ensures adaptability is not at odds with discipline, but rather, is managed within a structured framework.

*Leighton Wiggins:*

2. **What specific changes will you make to ensure that dissenting or minority perspectives are addressed earlier and more robustly in future decision-making rounds?**
- I will introduce a pre-meeting survey to surface dissent or alternative proposals in advance, ensuring these are part of the initial agenda. All proposals will include a “minority scenario” section, and I will facilitate early discussion of these perspectives before moving to consensus-building.
