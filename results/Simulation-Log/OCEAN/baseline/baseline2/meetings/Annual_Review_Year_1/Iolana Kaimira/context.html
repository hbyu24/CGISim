1. *Enhancing responsiveness to changing market opportunities while maintaining disciplined risk management:*

I will propose explicit “trigger criteria” (such as a set threshold for outperformance by an alternative asset) that, if met, will prompt a formal reconsideration of our investment allocation. This ensures we remain disciplined but are also ready to pivot when justified by evidence.

2. *Ensuring minority viewpoints are surfaced and addressed earlier:*
I will introduce a dedicated “minority perspectives” round early in each meeting, where all shareholders are explicitly invited to articulate any reservations or alternative strategies before consensus begins to form. **Hyejin Olatunde:**
1. *Ensuring dissenting/minority viewpoints are thoroughly explored before consensus:*
I will formalize a process where, in the first discussion round, each shareholder is asked to identify at least one potential risk or alternative approach, regardless of their initial support for the prevailing option. This will ensure dissenting views are not only welcomed but required, fostering richer debate and more robust decisions.

2. *Enhancing proactive risk monitoring and responsiveness to market conditions:*
I will work with the group to define clear, quantitative risk monitoring criteria—such as return differentials, volatility spikes, or macroeconomic shifts—that will be reviewed at each quarterly meeting. If these indicators cross predetermined thresholds, we will trigger an explicit discussion about whether our current strategy remains optimal.

**Heleen Suryaputra:**
1. *Enhancing early integration of minority/dissenting perspectives:*
In addition to the measures above, I will distribute pre-meeting materials that include a section specifically prompting shareholders to prepare and submit alternative viewpoints or concerns in advance. This will allow us to surface and integrate these perspectives from the outset.

2. *Refining and operationalizing risk monitoring criteria:*
I will propose a formal risk review framework that includes specific metrics (e.g., relative asset performance, deviation from expected returns, external market indicators) and clear decision thresholds. These will be documented and tracked through a quarterly risk dashboard, ensuring transparency and accountability.

**Saida Ewurum:**
1. *Improving responsiveness to market shifts/maximizing returns when alternatives outperform:*
Beyond the above, I will ensure that our quarterly review includes a retrospective analysis of “missed opportunities” to identify patterns and refine our criteria for switching assets. This will help us learn from past quarters and adapt more effectively in real time.

2. *Ensuring minority viewpoints and alternative strategies are surfaced earlier:*
I will encourage pre-meeting submission of alternative proposals and ensure these are discussed as a formal agenda item before any consensus-building begins.

**Iolana Kaimira:**
1. *Formalizing/operationalizing the structured quarterly risk review process for adaptability/accountability:*
I will codify our risk review process in a written policy, detailing the specific metrics to be tracked, the frequency of reviews, and the decision-making protocols when predefined thresholds are met. This policy will be reviewed annually for continuous improvement.

2. *Encouraging earlier/more diverse expression of minority viewpoints for decision robustness:*
I will incorporate structured “minority voice” rounds and assign, on a rotating basis, a “devil’s advocate” to challenge prevailing assumptions, ensuring a diversity of perspectives is always considered.
