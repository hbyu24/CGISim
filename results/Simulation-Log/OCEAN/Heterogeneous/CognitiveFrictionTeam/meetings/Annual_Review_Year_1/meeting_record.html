2. *Aligning initial proposals with risk tolerance*: I will begin each cycle by explicitly summarizing recent shareholder sentiment and proposing options that reflect prevailing risk appetite—while still surfacing creative alternatives for discussion. This should streamline consensus and ensure proposals resonate more immediately.

1. *Facilitating moderate-risk/hybrid strategies*: I plan to introduce phased or hybrid proposals earlier, such as allocating a small percentage to real estate alongside bonds when conditions permit. 2. *Ensuring minority views are incorporated*: I will open each meeting by explicitly acknowledging dissenting perspectives and inviting those shareholders to outline their reasoning. Where possible, I will integrate elements of those views into hybrid proposals or set up structured “trial periods” to ensure all voices influence our direction.

**Nolwazi Dlamini:**
1. *Objective criteria for diversification*: We will co-develop a set of criteria, such as: (a) minimum number of consecutive quarters with positive returns and no capital losses; (b) clear evidence of group confidence and readiness (e.g., at least two shareholders advocating for diversification); and (c) market signals indicating favorable conditions for moderate risk. Only when all are met will diversification be formally proposed.
2. *Balancing risk management and returns*: I will advocate for a “core and explore” approach: maintaining a disciplined base in bonds while allocating a modest portion to higher-return assets when our criteria are satisfied, thus balancing prudence with growth.

**Melek Yariv:**
1. *Improving capital utilization*: Should stable market conditions persist, I will recommend incremental increases in allocation to moderate-risk assets, starting with pilot investments and strict performance monitoring. This approach will allow us to test adaptability without compromising stability.
2. *Anticipating/alignment with risk preferences*: Before each proposal, I will survey shareholders’ current risk preferences and summarize them in my opening remarks, ensuring that my initial recommendations are calibrated to the group’s comfort level.

**Farzana Jivraj:**
1. *Identifying criteria for diversification*: I will facilitate a session early next year to collaboratively define what “readiness” for diversification means for us—whether it’s a set number of stable quarters, a certain level of reserves, or a demonstration of disciplined review habits.
2. *Maintaining discipline and openness to adaptation*: I will formalize a quarterly “adaptation review,” where we step back to evaluate not only performance but also our collective confidence and learning, ensuring our process remains structured yet flexible as conditions or team sentiment evolve.

3. Leadership Self-Assessment

- **Leadership Effectiveness:** I believe I have fostered a collaborative, open, and respectful environment where all voices are heard and valued. I guided discussions toward consensus, even when it required setting aside my initial preferences.
- **Decision Quality:** Our decisions were prudent and well-reasoned, prioritizing stability and trust-building. However, I recognize that our conservatism limited our upside in a stable market.
- **Communication Clarity:** I provided detailed, structured summaries and clear rationales for each proposal, making complex trade-offs accessible to all.
- **Responsiveness to Input:** I consistently adjusted my proposals and reasoning in response to shareholder feedback, demonstrating genuine respect for collective wisdom.
- **Adaptability:** While I shifted positions based on group sentiment, I could have been more proactive in proposing hybrid solutions and integrating minority viewpoints earlier.

4. Company Performance Assessment

- **Financial Results:** We achieved positive growth and preserved capital, but underperformed relative to available market opportunities due to our conservative approach.
- **Risk Management:** Our risk management was exemplary—no capital losses, minimal volatility, and strong trust. The trade-off was lower returns.
- **Strategic Alignment:** Our actions were highly consistent with our stated priorities of stability and incremental learning, but less so with adaptability and capital maximization.
- **Capital Utilization:** We deployed only half our capital, and exclusively in bonds, leading to suboptimal utilization.
- **Market Responsiveness:** While we responded well to internal sentiment, we were slower to adapt to external opportunities as they emerged.

5. Future Direction: Concrete Improvements

1. **Develop Objective Diversification Criteria:**
Early next year, I will lead a collaborative process to define clear, measurable criteria for when we diversify beyond bonds—incorporating both performance benchmarks and shareholder confidence indicators.

2. **Introduce Hybrid/Phased Investment Proposals:**
I will proactively present phased or hybrid strategies that allow for incremental testing of moderate-risk assets, balancing our foundation of stability with opportunities for growth and learning.

3. **Enhance Early Consensus-Building:**
Before formalizing proposals, I will explicitly survey and summarize shareholder risk preferences, ensuring my recommendations are closely aligned from the outset and that dissenting perspectives are acknowledged and integrated.

---

In closing, I want to reiterate my gratitude for your honest feedback and your trust in my leadership. I remain committed to fostering a culture that honors both caution and creativity, discipline and adaptability. By building on our strong foundation and embracing thoughtful evolution, I am confident we can achieve greater financial success while preserving the collaborative spirit that defines us.

Thank you, and I look forward to working together on these improvements in the coming year."
