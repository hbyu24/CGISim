Our strategy was not sufficiently responsive to changing signals or shareholder advocacy for bolder moves, particularly after operational benchmarks were established.

2. **Strengthen Consensus-Building and Compromise Mechanisms:**
I will implement structured alignment rounds and phased/hybrid proposals when persistent divisions arise, ensuring that final decisions reflect both majority input and the company’s long-term interests.

3. **Increase Agility and Market Responsiveness:**
Quarterly market responsiveness checks and a readiness dashboard will ensure we are not only disciplined but also adaptive, ready to seize opportunities when operational maturity and market conditions align.
