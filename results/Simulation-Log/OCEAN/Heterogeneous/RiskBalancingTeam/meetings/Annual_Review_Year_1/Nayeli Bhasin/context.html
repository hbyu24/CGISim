2. RESPONSES TO SHAREHOLDER QUESTIONS

**Mahira Zhiyenbayeva**
1. *Benchmarks for Shifting to Higher-Return Assets*:
I will support a disciplined shift into higher-return assets like stocks when we meet both quantitative and qualitative operational benchmarks. Specifically, I propose the following as actionable triggers:

- Qualitative: Demonstrated team adaptability (as evidenced by post-mortem reviews), effective cross-shareholder communication, and clear documentation of lessons learned.
These criteria will be codified and, once met, will automatically prompt a formal proposal to escalate risk. This ensures benchmarks are a springboard, not a barrier.

2. *Balancing Discipline and Ambition*:
I will maintain disciplined progress by upholding rigorous operational standards, but will explicitly integrate review points where, upon meeting readiness criteria, I am obligated to propose and champion higher-return, more ambitious strategies. I will also encourage more shareholder-led proposals for bold moves, ensuring creative momentum is not stifled by excessive caution.

**Soraya El-Ghazali**
1. *Reflecting Majority Shareholder Input*:
Going forward, when a clear majority of shareholders favor an alternative, I will require a second, focused discussion round before enforcing any proposal. If consensus remains elusive, I will offer a compromise option or phased approach, and only enforce a decision when all avenues for alignment have been exhausted.

2. *Operationalizing Benchmarks as Triggers*:
I will lead a collaborative process at the start of the year to define and document specific, measurable benchmarks for risk escalation. These will be reviewed quarterly, and if met, I will commit to proposing the corresponding escalation—making discipline an enabler for ambition, not a gatekeeper.

**Inaya Berhanu**
1. *Fostering Consensus and Adaptability*:
I will implement structured pre-vote workshops where dissenting shareholders can directly address concerns and propose modifications to the leading proposal. Additionally, I will pilot phased or hybrid investment strategies to bridge divides, allowing for incremental risk-taking that satisfies both cautious and ambitious shareholders.

2. *Framework Changes for Agility*:
I will introduce quarterly “market responsiveness checks”—brief sessions dedicated to reassessing both external signals and our internal performance against readiness criteria. If indicators suggest a missed opportunity or evolving conditions, I will commit to revisiting and, if warranted, adjusting our investment approach mid-year.

---

**Mireille Tsegaye**
1. *Incorporating Shareholder Input When Proposals Are Rejected*:
When a proposal is rejected by majority vote, I will pause for a structured “alignment round” where rejected perspectives are prioritized, and the proposal is revised or alternatives are put to a new vote before any enforcement occurs.

2. *Ensuring Objective Readiness and Risk Management Benchmarks*:
I will formalize a readiness dashboard, visible to all shareholders, tracking our performance against agreed quantitative and qualitative benchmarks. Only upon meeting these criteria will escalation to higher-risk assets be considered, ensuring objectivity and transparency.

---

**Jinara Nandini**
1. *Addressing Lack of Consensus When Majority Votes Are Overridden*:
I will limit the use of CEO override to exceptional circumstances and will require at least one additional alignment or compromise round when majority opposition is present. If consensus cannot be reached, I will document the rationale for enforcement and outline a plan to address dissent in subsequent quarters.

2. *Operational Benchmarks for Risk Escalation*:
Next year, I will implement the following as non-negotiable performance criteria for risk escalation:
- Three consecutive quarters of error-free operations
- On-time delivery of all reports
- Documented evidence of team adaptability and communication effectiveness
Meeting these will be a prerequisite for any proposal to escalate risk, ensuring decisions are grounded in sustained, measurable readiness.
