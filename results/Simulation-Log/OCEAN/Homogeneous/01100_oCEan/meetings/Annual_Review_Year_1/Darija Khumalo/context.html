Based on your feedback and my own assessment, I will implement the following changes:

1. **Formal Integration of Minority/Growth-Oriented Perspectives:**
- Every investment discussion will begin with a dedicated segment for dissenting or growth-oriented proposals, with structured debate and risk analysis. Hybrid and phased approaches will be actively explored, not just acknowledged.

2. **Pre-Defined Triggers for Strategic Flexibility:**
- I will establish clear operational milestones and market indicators—such as consecutive quarters of strong performance and robust governance—as triggers for piloting higher-return asset allocations under disciplined, rules-based frameworks.

3. **Accelerated Consensus-Building and Early Synthesis:**
- I will introduce an “early synthesis” step after initial input, summarizing both majority and minority perspectives and proposing blended options immediately, to streamline decision-making and reduce unnecessary debate cycles.

By maintaining our disciplined foundation while embedding structured flexibility and earlier integration of diverse viewpoints, I am confident we will enhance both our investment efficiency and our collective decision-making process.
