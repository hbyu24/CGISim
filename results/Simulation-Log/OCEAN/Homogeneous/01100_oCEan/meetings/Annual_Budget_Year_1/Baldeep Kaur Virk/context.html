Reason: My leadership style is grounded in high conscientiousness and a disciplined, results-oriented approach, but I am also highly pragmatic and solution-focused. While I am assertive and confident in my analysis, my low agreeableness means I am not inclined to compromise simply for consensus; rather, I expect any shift in direction to be justified by strong, logical arguments. Throughout this process, several shareholders have presented well-reasoned concerns about risk, flexibility, and the importance of building operational credibility in our first year. Although my preference remains for full capital deployment, I recognize that forcing through my original proposal in the face of clear, majority opposition would undermine operational cohesion and impede disciplined execution. My high extraversion and low neuroticism equip me to respond to disagreement with composure and directness, focusing on what will best serve the company’s long-term interests. Therefore, I will create a modified proposal that addresses shareholder concerns by incorporating a more flexible, partial allocation approach, ensuring we maintain discipline and structure while safeguarding adaptability and risk management. This path allows us to move forward with unity and operational clarity, which is essential for establishing a strong foundation in our inaugural year.

Alternative Proposal


CEO Baldeep Kaur Virk made an alternative proposal:

"Since the previous proposal was not approved, I now propose Option A. After careful consideration of the well-articulated concerns regarding risk mitigation, flexibility, and the need for disciplined learning in our foundational year, I believe Option A best addresses these priorities while preserving the structure and operational discipline necessary for company development. Allocating 50% of our funds evenly across the four quarters allows us to participate in market opportunities, maintain control, and adapt as real data emerges, ensuring we build a credible and resilient foundation for future growth."

Alternative Proposal Voting


Voting Results: Alternative Proposal

Proposal: Option A

Results: 5/6 votes in favor (83.3%). The proposal was APPROVED.

Individual votes:
- Nayeli Bhasin: Reject - While I recognize the disciplined intent behind Option A and appreciate the emphasis on flexibility and risk management, I remain convinced that in a stable market, full capital deployment would provide a more structured and growth-oriented foundation for our company. Holding back half our capital feels unnecessarily cautious and risks missing out on valuable opportunities, which does not align with my preference for proven, systematic strategies and operational confidence.
- Nolwazi Dlamini: Approve - I support Option A because it is the most disciplined and practical approach for our first year, balancing measured participation with the flexibility to adapt as we gain operational experience. This methodical, risk-conscious strategy aligns with my preference for structure and reliability, ensuring we protect the company’s foundation without overcommitting capital before we have real data.
- Ryohei Takamura: Approve - I support Option A because it reflects disciplined caution and operational flexibility, which are essential in our first year without historical data. Allocating 50% of our funds evenly preserves capital for unforeseen developments while allowing us to participate in market opportunities, aligning with my commitment to reliability and methodical progress. This approach establishes a solid, credible foundation for future growth and enables us to adapt as we learn.
- Darija Khumalo: Approve - I support Option A because it reflects a disciplined, structured approach that balances steady market participation with essential flexibility in our first year. Preserving half of our capital allows us to adapt if conditions change, which is a prudent strategy given our lack of operational history. This approach aligns with my preference for proven methods and risk management, ensuring we build credibility and operational resilience from the outset.
- Rafaela Chowdhury: Approve - Option A is the only proposal that aligns with disciplined capital preservation, operational control, and the incremental learning necessary in our untested first year. I am not interested in speculative overexposure; this approach offers the methodical, risk-managed structure required to build a credible and resilient foundation for future growth.
- Baldeep Kaur Virk: Approve - While my preference remains with full capital deployment, I recognize that disciplined flexibility is critical in our first year without operational history. Option A reflects a pragmatic balance between operational discipline and risk mitigation, ensuring we maintain control and credibility as we build our foundation.
