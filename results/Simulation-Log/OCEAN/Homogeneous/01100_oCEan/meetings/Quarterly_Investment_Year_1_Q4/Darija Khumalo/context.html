Bonds have delivered exactly what we require at this foundational stage: steady 5% returns with minimal volatility, directly supporting our priorities of capital preservation, operational control, and the credibility we are building with stakeholders. The consistency of bonds has enabled us to refine our processes, demonstrate disciplined execution, and establish a reliable operational track record—all of which are essential as we continue to mature as a company.

While I recognize the compelling arguments for pursuing higher returns through a disciplined, rules-based allocation to stocks, I remain convinced that it is premature to introduce additional volatility before we have further consolidated our credibility and gathered more operational data. Our governance structures and quarterly reviews are maturing, and I appreciate the ambition and forward-thinking perspectives brought to the table, particularly around signaling operational maturity. However, the majority’s well-reasoned support for bonds, combined with our current risk profile, makes a disciplined, risk-managed approach the most prudent course of action for this quarter.

I am confident that by maintaining our focus on bonds, we will continue to reinforce our reputation for reliability, protect our capital, and position ourselves for sustainable, long-term growth. I encourage all shareholders to support this proposal so we can move forward with clarity, discipline, and a unified commitment to the company’s ongoing success."
