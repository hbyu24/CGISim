I will distribute background materials and meeting agendas earlier, giving everyone more time to review and prepare questions. This should make our meetings more efficient and allow for deeper, more active participation.

**On responsiveness to changing market opportunities (Rimma, Veena):**
I will introduce a regular review of quarterly market trends and outcomes as part of our decision-making process, so we can identify when market conditions shift in ways that may justify a change in strategy.
