**Areas for Improvement:**
- Nearly all of you noted the need for clearer, more detailed preparatory materials and earlier invitations for questions and concerns. You expressed a desire to feel more prepared and engaged before meetings begin.
- There is a shared concern that we did not sufficiently explore alternative perspectives or adapt our investment strategy to changing quarterly market opportunities, especially when higher returns were available in stocks or real estate. Many of you asked for a more thorough analysis of risks and potential upside, and for greater responsiveness to market dynamics.

---

**2. Responses to Specific Questions:**

I have grouped similar questions together for clarity, while ensuring each is addressed:

**On balancing stability with higher returns in future years (Melek, Sabira, Rimma, Veena, Mikayla):**
Our collective preference for stability and capital preservation has served us well in building trust and avoiding losses. However, I recognize that this approach led us to capture only 27.2% of the maximum possible returns. In future years, I will facilitate more deliberate discussions about risk tolerance at the start of each planning cycle, encouraging us to revisit our comfort with calculated risk. I will also ensure that, when market conditions are stable or favorable, we explicitly consider allocating a portion of our investments to higher-return assets—such as stocks or real estate—when the risk is acceptable and the potential reward justifies it. This could mean, for example, piloting a small allocation to higher-yield assets in select quarters, while keeping the majority in bonds to maintain our safety net.

**On exploring alternative options and risks more thoroughly (Melek, Sabira, Rimma, Veena, Mikayla):**
Next year, I will provide more comprehensive preparatory materials before each meeting, including scenario analyses that show the potential outcomes and risks of each asset class in the current market context. I will also introduce a formal step in our agenda for surfacing and debating alternative viewpoints, and I will invite each shareholder to articulate not only their preferred option, but also any concerns about options not chosen. This will help ensure that we do not overlook either risks or opportunities.

**On improving preparatory communication and engagement (Sabira, Rimma, Veena, Mikayla):**
I will distribute background materials and meeting agendas earlier, giving everyone more time to review and prepare questions. I will also open a pre-meeting window for shareholders to submit questions or suggest discussion topics, which I will address at the start of each session. This should make our meetings more efficient and allow for deeper, more active participation.

**On responsiveness to changing market opportunities (Rimma, Veena):**
I will introduce a regular review of quarterly market trends and outcomes as part of our decision-making process, so we can identify when market conditions shift in ways that may justify a change in strategy. I will encourage the group to consider, each quarter, whether our current approach remains optimal or whether a tactical adjustment could improve results without compromising our core values.

---

**3. Leadership Self-Assessment:**
