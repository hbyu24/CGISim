After the proposal was rejected, CEO Tafadzwa Ndlovu decided to force through original proposal.
Reason: I have listened carefully to all perspectives and recognize the concerns raised about operational discipline and the preference for a more cautious approach. However, given the stable market and the need to set a clear, decisive tone for our company’s growth, I remain convinced that continuing with real estate is the most practical and results-driven path. My leadership style is direct and action-oriented—I prioritize momentum, visible progress, and learning through real-world execution rather than retreating to safer but less impactful options. While I respect the arguments for reliability, my responsibility as CEO is to act in the company’s best interests as I see them. I am confident that, if we adhere to proven routines and maintain a practical, focused approach, we are capable of managing the operational demands of real estate. This is my final decision for this quarter: we will proceed with investing in real estate, and I expect everyone to commit fully to disciplined execution as we move forward.
