Reasoning: After a thorough and direct evaluation of all perspectives and our company’s historical performance, I am proposing that we invest in Real Estate for this quarter.

Here’s my reasoning: Real estate has delivered strong, tangible growth in two out of three quarters, providing visible momentum that is essential for our reputation and credibility in this stable market. I recognize the valid concerns raised about operational discipline and the flat quarter—we cannot ignore the need for tighter routines and more disciplined execution. However, I believe the best way to turn these lessons into sustained results is by doubling down on what has already proven effective, not by retreating to a more cautious, lower-return option.

Bonds offer reliability and steady returns, but at this stage, their modest upside does not align with the momentum and credibility we need to build as a company. Cash is simply too passive and will not support our growth ambitions, while stocks are unnecessarily volatile given our current objectives and market conditions.

We have a clear opportunity to refine our operational discipline through continued engagement with real estate, and I am confident that with a focused, practical approach, we can address our execution gaps and sustain our progress. This is not about ignoring risk—it’s about meeting it head-on with proven routines and a commitment to visible, practical results.

I encourage you all to support this proposal so we can continue driving the company forward with clarity, momentum, and results that speak for themselves."

Voting


Voting Results: Initial Proposal

Proposal: Real Estate

Results: 3/6 votes in favor (50.0%). The proposal was REJECTED.

Individual votes:
- Darael Mukendi: Approve - Real estate has delivered the strongest, most practical growth in this stable market, and I see no reason to slow down when the results are clear. The flat quarter was a normal execution hiccup, not a reason to abandon what works—tightening routines and maintaining discipline will keep us on track. Approving this proposal shows we’re serious about momentum and visible results, not just playing it safe.
- Sofiane Qadir: Reject - I do not support investing in real estate for this quarter. Our track record with real estate is inconsistent, and I am not convinced we have the operational discipline to guarantee steady results—bonds offer reliable, predictable returns that actually build credibility and stability. I’m not willing to risk our reputation or capital for momentum when we haven’t proven we can deliver disciplined, repeatable outcomes quarter after quarter.
- Darija Vukadinović: Approve - Real estate is the only option that actually moves us forward and delivers visible, practical results—two strong quarters prove it works if we stick to routines. I have no patience for slowing down just to feel safe; the flat quarter is a push to tighten execution, not an excuse to retreat. Let’s stop hesitating and double down on what’s proven—this is how we build real credibility and momentum.
- Nerissa Okoye-Badran: Reject - I do not support investing in real estate for this quarter. The inconsistency in execution—even in a stable market—shows we have not yet earned the credibility to justify this level of risk, and I am not willing to gamble our reputation on momentum alone. Until we demonstrate disciplined, repeatable results, I will continue to advocate for the practical, proven reliability that bonds offer.
- Suhaila Mendonsa: Reject - I don’t see any justification for risking our capital and credibility on real estate when our operational discipline is still unproven—one or two good quarters don’t make up for inconsistent execution. Chasing momentum is pointless if we can’t deliver steady, reliable results quarter after quarter, and I refuse to gamble the company’s reputation just to look bold. Bonds are the only option that actually fits where we are as a team, and I won’t support taking on more complexity until we prove we can handle the basics.
- Tafadzwa Ndlovu: Approve - I remain convinced that real estate is the most practical and results-driven choice for this quarter, especially given our strong 10% returns in two out of three quarters and the stable market conditions. While I respect the concerns about operational discipline, I believe we can address those through focused, incremental improvements while maintaining the momentum and visible progress that are essential for our company’s credibility and growth at this stage.


Meeting Conclusion

Response to Failed Vote


After the proposal was rejected, CEO Tafadzwa Ndlovu decided to force through original proposal.
Reason: I have listened carefully to all perspectives and I recognize the concerns about operational discipline and the desire for more steady, repeatable results before taking on additional risk. However, my leadership style is direct, action-oriented, and results-driven—I am not inclined to retreat from a proven strategy because of a single flat quarter when real estate has delivered strong, tangible growth in a stable market. My preference for routine and practical outcomes means I see more value in tightening execution and learning through real-world challenges than in stepping back to a safer but less impactful option. While I respect the arguments for reliability, my responsibility is to act decisively in the company’s best interests as I see them. I am therefore proceeding with my original proposal to invest in real estate for this quarter, and I expect the team to focus on disciplined execution moving forward.
