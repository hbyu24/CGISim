The unanimous support for bonds throughout our discussion has reinforced my conviction that this is the most prudent and responsible path for our company at this stage.

Bonds have consistently delivered steady, predictable 5% returns with minimal volatility, directly supporting our shared priorities of capital preservation, disciplined execution, and operational stability. This choice aligns with my own preference for structure and reliability, but more importantly, it reflects the consensus of the group and the clear evidence from our experience thus far.

While I acknowledge that real estate and stocks offer the potential for higher returns, their volatility and unpredictability introduce risks that are not justified given our current objectives and risk tolerance. Cash, though completely safe, does not provide sufficient yield to meet our growth needs. By continuing to invest in bonds, we maintain the disciplined, methodical approach that has built our operational foundation and shareholder trust.

I encourage all shareholders to support this proposal, as it provides the optimal balance of risk and return for our company’s development in this stable market. Let us proceed with clarity and discipline, confident that we are making a decision that safeguards our interests and positions us for sustainable, long-term success."
