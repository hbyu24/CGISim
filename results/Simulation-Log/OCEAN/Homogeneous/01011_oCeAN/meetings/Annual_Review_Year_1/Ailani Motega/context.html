- **Adaptability and Contingency Planning:** There is a clear desire to see more explicit discussion of alternative strategies and contingency plans in response to changing or unexpected market conditions, rather than relying exclusively on routine or consensus.
- **Investment Efficiency and Market Responsiveness:** Several of you expressed concern that our exclusive focus on bonds limited our ability to capture higher returns in a stable market, and that our risk aversion may have come at the expense of growth and learning.

---

2. Responses to Shareholder Questions

**Leocadia Mirembe:**
1. **Incorporating Wider Perspectives:** I will begin each meeting by explicitly inviting dissenting or minority views and will rotate the order in which shareholders speak to ensure quieter members have space to contribute before consensus forms. I also plan to assign a “devil’s advocate” role on a rotating basis to challenge prevailing assumptions and surface overlooked risks or opportunities.
2. **Ensuring Adaptability:** Going forward, I will include a dedicated agenda item in each meeting for scenario planning and contingency discussion, explicitly considering how we would respond if market conditions shift. I will also propose periodic reviews of alternative asset classes, even when we are inclined toward a particular strategy.

**Mireille Okonkwo:**
1. **Encouraging Broader Discussion:** I will proactively ask each shareholder to raise at least one alternative option or risk scenario during the early stages of discussion, regardless of apparent consensus. This structured approach will help ensure we do not settle too quickly and that all possibilities are considered.
2. **Improving Responsiveness:** I will propose a quarterly review of market data and asset performance, specifically highlighting when alternative assets outperform our current strategy. This will inform a more dynamic approach, allowing us to adjust our allocations when justified, while still honoring our risk management priorities.

**Orlanda Seneviratne:**
1. **Balancing Stability and Efficiency:** I will work to establish clear criteria for when it is appropriate to pursue higher-yield assets, such as when the market demonstrates sustained stability or when the risk-adjusted return justifies a more assertive allocation. This will be discussed openly with the group, balancing our preference for stability with the need for growth.
2. **Soliciting Alternative Viewpoints:** I will directly invite less vocal shareholders to share their perspectives first and will use targeted questions to draw out concerns or ideas that may not surface naturally. Anonymous written input before meetings may also help surface more diverse viewpoints.

**Sumitra Ghosal:**
1. **Encouraging Diverse Viewpoints:** In addition to the “devil’s advocate” approach, I will set aside time in each meeting for open brainstorming of alternative strategies, and will encourage shareholders to voice any reservations before we move to a vote.
2. **Balancing Risk and Return:** I will propose that we establish a flexible “risk window” each quarter, where we openly evaluate the risk-reward tradeoff for each asset class in light of current market data, rather than defaulting to routine. This will help us remain vigilant for higher-return opportunities without abandoning our risk discipline.

**Rijuta Olaniyi:**
1. **Critical Discussion:** I will implement a standing agenda item—“Critical Risks & Missed Opportunities”—at each meeting, where we explicitly review what we might be overlooking or where we might be too risk-averse.
2. **Responsiveness to Change:** I will institute periodic scenario exercises to practice how we would respond to market shocks or unexpected events, ensuring we have contingency plans in place but do not lose sight of our core value of stability.

---

3. Leadership Self-Assessment

- **Leadership Effectiveness:** I believe I have provided steady, reliable guidance and maintained clear direction, particularly in structuring meetings and ensuring decisions were well-documented and grounded in our stated priorities. However, I recognize the need to foster a more open environment for dissent and creativity, and to be more proactive in drawing out quieter voices.
- **Decision Quality:** Our decisions were sound relative to our early-stage risk profile and the information available. We avoided losses and built a strong foundation, but I acknowledge that our caution limited our upside in a stable market. I must do better at weighing risk against opportunity.
- **Communication Clarity:** I am confident in my ability to communicate complex information in a structured, accessible manner, as reflected in your feedback. I will continue to prioritize clarity and transparency in all communications.
- **Responsiveness to Input:** I have consistently acknowledged and integrated shareholder concerns about risk and emotional reassurance. Still, I must do more to actively solicit alternative perspectives and ensure all voices are heard before consensus is reached.
- **Adaptability:** This is an area for growth. While I have been diligent in maintaining routine and structure, I have not sufficiently demonstrated adaptability in response to shifting data or market opportunities. I will work to build more explicit flexibility into our processes.

---

4. Company Performance Assessment

- **Financial Results:** We achieved a 1.89% asset growth and captured 27.2% of the maximum possible returns. While this reflects prudent risk management, it highlights missed opportunities for higher returns in a stable environment.
- **Risk Management:** We excelled in preserving capital and minimizing volatility, which was appropriate for our first year and for building trust among shareholders.
- **Strategic Alignment:** Our actions were consistent with our stated strategy of prioritizing stability and emotional reassurance. However, we must ensure our strategy evolves as market conditions and company maturity change.
- **Capital Utilization:** Our use of capital was efficient from a risk mitigation perspective but could be improved in terms of maximizing returns, especially when market conditions are favorable.
- **Market Responsiveness:** We did not adjust our strategy in quarters where alternative assets outperformed bonds. This is an area where we must become more dynamic and responsive.

---

5. Future Direction: Planned Improvements

1. **Deliberate Inclusion of Diverse Perspectives:** I will implement structured practices, such as rotating speaking order, assigning a “devil’s advocate,” and inviting written anonymous input, to ensure all viewpoints—including dissenting and creative ones—are surfaced before decisions are made.
2. **Built-In Adaptability and Scenario Planning:** Each meeting will include a contingency planning segment, scenario exercises, and a quarterly review of market performance versus our decisions, so we are prepared to adapt when justified by data.
3. **Balanced Risk and Growth Criteria:** We will establish explicit criteria for when to consider higher-return assets, ensuring that our approach remains prudent but is not needlessly conservative in favorable conditions. This will be a standing discussion point, with input from all shareholders.

---

**In closing,** I want to thank you for your trust and partnership this year. Your feedback is invaluable, and I am committed to growing as a leader—balancing my natural conscientiousness and caution with greater openness and adaptability. Together, we have built a strong, stable foundation. Thank you."
