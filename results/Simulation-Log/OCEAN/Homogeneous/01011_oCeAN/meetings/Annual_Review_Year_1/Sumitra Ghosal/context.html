- Leocadia Mirembe: 2/5 - The company somewhat underperformed market opportunities, capturing only 27.2% of the maximum possible returns despite a stable environment favorable to higher growth.
- Mireille Okonkwo: 2/5 - The company somewhat underperformed market opportunities, capturing only 27.2% of maximum possible returns despite a stable market environment.
- Orlanda Seneviratne: 2/5 - The company achieved modest growth and preserved capital but captured only 27.2% of available returns, underperforming relative to market opportunities in the stable environment.
- Sumitra Ghosal: 2/5 - The company somewhat underperformed market opportunities, capturing only 27.2% of potential returns despite the stable environment.
- Rijuta Olaniyi: 2/5 - The company achieved some asset growth but significantly underperformed market opportunities, capturing only 27.2% of potential returns due to a highly risk-averse strategy.

Risk Management: 5.0/5

- Leocadia Mirembe: 5/5 - Risk was managed exceptionally well, with all decisions prioritizing capital preservation and avoiding significant losses.
- Mireille Okonkwo: 5/5 - Risk was managed excellently, with all decisions prioritizing capital preservation and minimizing volatility throughout the year.
- Orlanda Seneviratne: 5/5 - The company maintained an excellent balance of risk and return, prioritizing capital preservation and minimizing volatility across all investment decisions.
- Sumitra Ghosal: 5/5 - The company demonstrated excellent risk-reward balance, consistently prioritizing capital preservation and avoiding significant losses across all quarters.
- Rijuta Olaniyi: 5/5 - Risk was managed exceptionally well, with all investment choices minimizing volatility and preserving capital across all quarters.

Strategic Alignment: 5.0/5

- Leocadia Mirembe: 5/5 - Investment choices were perfectly aligned with the stated strategy of stability and emotional reassurance throughout the year.
- Mireille Okonkwo: 5/5 - Investment choices were in perfect alignment with the company’s stated strategy of prioritizing stability, capital preservation, and emotional reassurance.
- Orlanda Seneviratne: 5/5 - Investment choices were perfectly aligned with the company’s stated strategy of stability and risk aversion, consistently supporting the agreed-upon approach.
- Sumitra Ghosal: 5/5 - Investment choices perfectly aligned with the company’s stated strategy of stability and caution throughout the year.
- Rijuta Olaniyi: 5/5 - Investment decisions were in perfect alignment with our stated strategy of capital preservation and stability, with no significant deviations.

Capital Utilization: 3.0/5

- Leocadia Mirembe: 3/5 - Capital was deployed with average efficiency, as the exclusive focus on bonds left some growth potential untapped.
- Mireille Okonkwo: 3/5 - Capital utilization was average; while funds were deployed efficiently for risk mitigation, higher-yield opportunities were not fully pursued.
- Orlanda Seneviratne: 3/5 - Capital was deployed efficiently from a risk mitigation perspective, but the exclusive focus on bonds limited the company’s ability to maximize returns in favorable conditions.
- Sumitra Ghosal: 3/5 - Capital utilization was average, with efficient risk mitigation but limited pursuit of higher returns when market conditions were favorable.
- Rijuta Olaniyi: 3/5 - Capital was utilized with average efficiency, as the cautious approach left considerable potential returns untapped in a stable market.

Market Responsiveness: 2.8/5

- Leocadia Mirembe: 2/5 - The company was slow to adapt to market conditions, maintaining a static approach even when alternative assets outperformed bonds.
- Mireille Okonkwo: 3/5 - The company demonstrated adequate responsiveness to major market shifts but did not sufficiently adapt strategy in quarters when higher-return assets outperformed bonds.
- Orlanda Seneviratne: 3/5 - The company maintained a disciplined process but showed only adequate responsiveness to market signals, with limited willingness to adjust strategy when alternative assets outperformed bonds.
- Sumitra Ghosal: 3/5 - The company was adequately responsive to major market shifts, but did not adjust strategy to take advantage of outperforming assets in certain quarters.
- Rijuta Olaniyi: 3/5 - The company demonstrated adequate responsiveness to major market shifts but did not adjust strategy to capitalize on higher-return opportunities when available.

Overall Company Rating: 3.6/5

Meeting Conclusion


CEO Ailani Motega concluded the meeting:

"Ailani Motega Thank you all for your candid feedback and for the trust you have placed in my leadership this year. I am encouraged by your recognition of our strengths in clarity, risk management, and strategic alignment, though I take seriously the need to improve our adaptability and to more actively draw out diverse perspectives. In the coming year, I commit to fostering richer discussion by explicitly inviting alternative viewpoints, integrating scenario planning into our process, and establishing clear criteria for adapting our investment approach when conditions warrant. My focus will remain on providing steady, organized leadership while embracing greater openness to constructive change. Together, I am confident we can build on our foundation of stability to achieve stronger growth, resilience, and shared success in the years ahead."
