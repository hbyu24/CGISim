2. **Enhanced Scenario Analysis and Ambition Calibration:** Embed rigorous scenario analysis and explicit opportunity cost discussions into our evaluation process, so we regularly weigh both the safest and most ambitious options against market conditions and team readiness.

3. **Institutionalized Emotional Safety Mechanisms:** Formalize regular emotional check-ins, standing agenda items for minority perspectives, and periodic “ambition check-ins” to ensure psychological safety remains robust even as we pursue higher returns.
