Final Proposal


CEO Zuleikha Berhane made the following proposal:

"Vote Proposal: Bonds
Reasoning: After careful consideration of all perspectives shared throughout our discussion, I am proposing that we invest in Bonds for this quarter.

My reasoning is grounded in both our historical experience and the clear consensus that has emerged among the majority of shareholders. Bonds have consistently delivered predictable, modest returns with minimal risk, which aligns closely with our company’s foundational values of stability, routine, and psychological comfort. Over the past two quarters, our investments in bonds have produced steady gains without any losses, reinforcing my confidence that this approach supports both capital preservation and responsible progress.

I fully acknowledge the concerns raised by shareholders who advocate for cash as the only option offering absolute safety and complete psychological reassurance. I understand that for some, even minimal risk exposure can create persistent anxiety and gradually erode trust and participation. These concerns are valid and have been carefully weighed in my decision. However, I believe that, in the context of our stable market environment and the need to demonstrate steady growth, bonds offer the best compromise. They allow us to maintain our disciplined approach, avoid stagnation, and build confidence through routine, while still protecting our foundation.

To address the ongoing concerns about risk, I commit to maintaining vigilant oversight and transparent communication regarding our investments. I will ensure that any changes in market conditions are promptly addressed and that all shareholders are kept informed, so that psychological comfort and trust remain at the center of our process.

In summary, bonds provide the optimal balance between capital preservation, modest growth, and psychological comfort for our company at this stage. I urge all shareholders to support this proposal, confident that it best positions us for continued stability, resilience, and responsible development."
